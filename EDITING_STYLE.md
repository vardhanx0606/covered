# Editing Style — FLASHOOT / Covered

Based on 16 reference edits (weddings, haldi/pasupu, pooja, saree store promo, bike shoot, concert, event teasers).

## Format
- **Vertical 9:16 (720x1280)** for most reels; some 16:9 / 4:3 for cinematic pieces.
- **Length: 23–35 s** (sweet spot ~30 s). Long-form (~78 s) only for product/cinematic shoots.
- 30 fps.
- Small **FLASHOOT watermark top-center** on every frame.

## Pacing (measured)
| Type | Avg shot length | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Hype / party / sangeet / concert | **0.5–0.9 s** | Rithika-Veer, Ibrahimpatnam wedding, saree store, concert |
| Couple / pre-wedding / event coverage | **1.2–1.8 s** | Gurunayak colony, clip.2, pooja |
| Cinematic / bridal / product | **2.5–4.5 s** | Vijayawada bridal, Kavya, Royal Enfield |
| Moment / family | **8–9 s** (near one-take) | Maya moments |

## Structure (the signature)
1. **Flash-burst intro** — first 0.5–1.7 s has rapid 1–3 frame cuts (0.1 s each). Hooks instantly.
2. **Beat-locked section** — cuts on every beat (~0.45–0.55 s apart ≈ 110–130 BPM).
3. **Breather hold** — one 3–5 s shot (usually the hero moment: couple, bride, dance).
4. **Second build** — beat cuts again, often double-time near the drop.
5. **Outro** — slow hold or fade to black / logo / text card (e.g. “TOMORROW 7:30PM…”).

## Transitions
- **White flash / light leak** (overexposed frame between shots).
- **Whip / motion-blur** transitions on dance and spin shots.
- **Fade through black** for cinematic pieces and to end.
- Mostly **hard cuts on beat** — transitions are accents, not every cut.
- Occasional **zoom-out-to-frame** (shot shrinks inside black, then cuts back full-screen).

## Shots & look
- Mix of **wide establishing → mid → tight detail** (mehendi hands, jewelry, saree weave, decor, pooja items).
- Lots of **detail/decor B-roll** to open (flowers, props, signage).
- Candid laughter and dance > posed.
- Color: **warm, saturated, punchy** for events; **golden/amber, soft contrast** for bridal cinematic; **moody/dark, lifted blacks** for bike/product.
- Natural handheld motion kept (iPhone-style), not over-stabilized.

## Text / graphics
- Minimal. Small serif-italic lyric/caption lines for cinematic, bold sans title cards for promos/teasers.
- Custom name logos (e.g. Telugu “Pasupu” script, #RithiKaVeer) placed bottom-center.
- Client branding (store logo) as end card.

## Audio
- Trending song / beat-driven track; every major cut lands on a beat or snare.
- Intro flash-burst synced to a riser or first hit.
